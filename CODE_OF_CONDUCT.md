# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the AROP community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Respect security embargoes and private incident handling.
- Do not publish private information, credentials, customer data, or non-public vulnerability details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sustained disruption, doxxing, sexualized attention, and retaliation are not tolerated in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports should be made privately through the channel documented in `SECURITY.md`. The initial enforcement contacts will be named before the repository is opened for public contribution.

Enforcement decisions should be prompt, proportionate, documented privately, and handled with respect for the reporter's confidentiality.
